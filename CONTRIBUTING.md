# Contributing

Suggestions are welcome. The list is kept small on purpose.

## The test

An entry belongs if it does at least one of these:

- gives you something to **manipulate**: a live model, a simulation, a figure with controls
- **tests** what you know: retrieval questions, practice problems, review over time

Tools that only explain, summarize, organize sources or navigate code are out of scope, however good they are.

## What to include

1. The project link and its author.
2. Its kind: interactive resource, agent skill, app or curriculum.
3. One learning goal it serves.
4. What it adds over a well-written prompt to a current model. Be specific.
5. Something a reader can try: a demo link, a prompt or an exercise.
6. Setup, and whether model access costs extra.
7. Whether you ran it yourself or only read its documentation.

Add the entry to the list in `README.md` as one line under the heading that fits its goal: linked name, one sentence, a setup tag, and `tried` or `read`. Mark that last one honestly.

## Attribution

Link to projects; do not copy their code. If you add an image or other asset from a project, include its source and license.
